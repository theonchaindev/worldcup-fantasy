# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Crypto-native football fans who hold WCF tokens. They check their fantasy team late at night, often on mobile, before or during a World Cup match. Adrenaline is already running. They are digitally fluent and have zero patience for slow, cluttered, or dated UIs. They want to feel like they're inside a live broadcast — fast data, clear decisions, no friction.

## Product Purpose

A token-gated World Cup fantasy football game on Solana. Users select a 15-player squad within a £100m budget, earn points from real World Cup match performances, and compete globally and in private leagues for a prize pool funded by entry fees and token rewards. Success looks like: a squad built in under 3 minutes, clear point progression during the tournament, and a leaderboard that makes competition feel real.

## Brand Personality

Intense · Fast · Competitive. The product should feel like a live sports broadcast — authoritative, high-contrast, every pixel earning its place. Not a casual game. Not a financial product. A serious competition.

## Anti-references

- **Old FPL / Dream11**: blocky, heavy borders, primary-color-heavy, dated sports app feel. No rainbow position colors clashing. No drop shadows on everything.
- **Crypto degen**: neon on black, aggressive gradients, laser-eyes energy, "wen moon" aesthetics. No glowing text, no gradient borders.
- **Cream / warm editorial**: the 2026 AI default warm-beige background, parchment feel, artisanal. None of that.
- **Generic SaaS dark mode**: purple gradients, glass cards everywhere, hero metrics (big number + small label + gradient accent), identical icon-card grids.

## Design Principles

1. **Type does the heavy lifting.** Hierarchy through scale and weight contrast, not decorative color. A well-set heading is more powerful than a glassmorphism card.
2. **Information earns its place.** Every data point shown should be actionable or contextual. No filler stats for visual weight.
3. **Competition is the brand.** The leaderboard, points, and rank should feel viscerally real — like a scoreboard, not a dashboard.
4. **Speed is respect.** No unnecessary animation. Motion has a job; when it has none, it ships as instant.
5. **Black is the field.** The pitch is dark. The product is dark. But contrast is the tool, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Reduced motion via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` on all transitions. Position colors must not be the sole indicator of meaning (always paired with text label).
